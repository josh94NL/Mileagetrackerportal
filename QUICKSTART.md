# Quick Start Guide

## Welcome to Mileage Tracker! 🚗

This app is designed to help you track your mileage in under 10 seconds per trip.

## First Steps

1. **Create an Account**
   - Click "Create account" on the landing page
   - Enter your name, email, and password
   - You'll be automatically logged in

2. **Add Your First Vehicle** (Optional but recommended)
   - Navigate to "Vehicles" in the bottom menu
   - Click "Add Vehicle"
   - Enter vehicle name, license plate, and current odometer
   - This enables auto-prefilling of odometer values

3. **Add Your First Trip**
   - Click the large "+" button in the center of the bottom nav
   - Date and time are pre-filled with today/now
   - Enter start and end locations
   - Enter odometer readings (or they'll auto-fill if you have a vehicle)
   - Distance calculates automatically
   - Choose Business or Private
   - Click "Save Trip"

## Speed Tips ⚡

### Quick Actions
- **Use current location**: Taps your GPS for end location
- **Same as last trip**: Fills start location from your last trip's end
- **Duplicate last trip**: Copies all fields from your last trip
- **Reverse start/end**: Swaps start and end locations

### Smart Defaults
- Date: Automatically today's date
- Time: Automatically current time
- Vehicle: Auto-selected if you only have one
- Odometer Start: Pulled from vehicle's current reading

### Keyboard Optimization
- Large numeric inputs for odometer
- Auto-focus on first field
- Mobile keyboards open automatically

1. Open in Safari
2. Tap the Share button
3. Select "Add to Home Screen"

**Android (Chrome)**:
1. Open in Chrome
2. Tap the three-dot menu
3. Select "Add to Home Screen"

## Workflow Example

**Scenario**: You just completed a business trip

1. Tap "+" button (center of bottom nav)
2. Date & time already filled ✓
3. Type start location: "Office"
4. Type end location: "Client Site"
5. Enter odometer start: 45000
6. Enter odometer end: 45120
7. Distance auto-calculates: 120 km ✓
8. Business already selected ✓
9. Tap "Save Trip"

**Total time**: ~8 seconds ⚡

## Tips for Fast Entry

1. **Keep odometer readings handy**: Take a quick photo before and after trips
2. **Use simple location names**: "Home", "Office", "Client A"
3. **Set up vehicles first**: Enables auto-filling
4. **Use quick actions**: "Same as last trip" for return journeys
